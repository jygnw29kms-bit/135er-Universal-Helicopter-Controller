# Preliminary BOM V0.1

This is the architecture-stage BOM and will be refined after the first CAD iteration.

## Controller

- 1x RP2350 development board / controller board
- 1x USB-C connection
- Wiring/connectors
- Optional CAN or RS485 transceivers for modular expansion

## Cyclic

- 2x contactless magnetic angle sensors
- 2x compatible diametric magnets
- 4x 608-2RS bearings minimum, final quantity TBD
- 8 mm steel shaft material
- 20-22 mm aluminium tube for cyclic stick
- M3 fasteners for sensors/electronics
- M4 fasteners for structural printed parts
- Optional light extension springs

## Collective

- 2x Hall / magnetic angle sensors
- Bearings and steel pivot shaft
- Aluminium tube
- Buttons/switches for governor, starter and auxiliary functions

## Pedals

- 1x yaw Hall / magnetic sensor
- Optional 2x toe-brake Hall sensors
- Steel main shaft
- Bearings
- M4 structural hardware

## Printing

Baseline material: PETG.
Higher-load alternatives may include ASA or reinforced engineering filament where appropriate.
